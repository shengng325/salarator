This is a comparison of two separate employment scenarios.
  • Singapore: citizen or PR at full CPF rates, age 55 or below. 2026 ordinary-wage ceiling S$8,000/month; employee 20%, employer 17%, with low-wage rules and monthly CPF rounding. Tax uses resident rates from YA 2024 onward, employee CPF relief and S$1,000 earned-income relief. Total personal relief is capped at S$80,000. No one-off tax rebates.
  • Malaysia: Malaysian citizen below 60; employee EPF estimated at 11%; employer 13% at wages up to RM5,000, otherwise 12%. These are percentage estimates; statutory KWSP contribution-table bands and rounding can differ from these figures.
  • Malaysia tax uses the published YA 2025 resident schedule, RM9,000 personal relief and employee EPF relief up to RM4,000. Progressive mode includes the RM400 individual rebate where chargeable income is at most RM35,000. Additional relief fields exclude those already included.
  • REP mode applies 15% to chargeable employment income after the same reliefs. Use only if approved and within your eligible REP period; this calculator does not determine eligibility.
  • SOCSO, EIS, insurance, zakat, other deductions and benefits are excluded. Take-home pay = gross − income tax − employee contribution. Total pay after tax incl CPF/EPF = take-home + both contributions (gross − income tax + employer contribution). Employee contributions are already part of gross.
